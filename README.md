Solmar Bay is an original open-world crime game set in a sun-soaked coastal city of the same name. You play Rico, a small-time driver who takes work from fixer Marisol Vega, keeps his wheels alive at Tito Ramos's garage, and tries to build a bankroll without spending his life in a cell. Drive the boulevard, run the five-mission story, dodge or fight the police across a five-star wanted system, take taxi fares, run the checkpoint race, and hunt twenty hidden packages from downtown rooftops to the end of the marina pier.

## Controls

- **W A S D** — walk / drive (W accelerates, S brakes and reverses)
- **Shift** — sprint on foot
- **Mouse** — orbit the camera (click the game to lock the pointer); arrow keys also turn the camera
- **E** — enter or leave the nearest car
- **F** — shoot your pistol
- **R** — reload
- **K** — confirm a garage repair when prompted
- **1 / 2 / 3** — buy at the Tienda Brisa counter (first aid / kevlar vest / pistol rounds)

## Features

- A full grid city from -280 to +280 with downtown, a beachfront boulevard and a marina whose pier is walkable to its very end
- Five story missions with goto, car, driving, fight, escape and hold objectives, paying $3,550 in total
- A 0–5 star wanted system: police cars pursue an intercept point, officers bail out and chase on foot, roadblocks and guards appear at four stars, and a bust meter fills when the law gets close
- Gang crews with shared cover search, flank anchors and cover / peek / advance / retreat behaviour
- Traffic that follows lanes, brakes for the car ahead and steers through corners in arcs; parked cars you can steal; crash damage that slows a wrecked car
- Taxi fares, a 12-checkpoint race worth $500, and 20 hidden packages at $25 each
- Three enterable interiors: a safehouse with a save point, Tito's Garage with a $40 repair pad, and the Tienda Brisa shop selling first aid, armour and ammunition
- Health and an armour pool, busted and wasted flows with fines and hospital bills, and a localStorage save that persists cash, armour, mission progress and found packages
- Canvas-painted facades and signs, two-segment drooping palms, an animated sea, warm late-afternoon light with ACES tone mapping, procedural WebAudio sound, a live minimap, and an adaptive quality scaler that keeps the frame budget honest

## Run it locally

```
node serve.ts
```

Then open the address it prints: http://localhost:8080

## Note

Solmar Bay is an original work made for this bounty. Every name, character, vehicle, business and story beat was invented for this game; it shares nothing with any commercial title beyond the broad idea of driving around a sunny city.
